# 0008 — promote trusts a release record that no longer describes the plane

- Status: open
- Labels: platform, core/promote.sh, core/launchpad.sh, safety
- Found: 2026-11-02, audit of core/ before the public launch

## Problem

`core/promote.sh` promotes when `.crew/release.json` says `held`. The record
can say `held` while the plane does not hold that canary: `demo-hold` ends
with its own rollback and leaves the record at `held`; a launchpad killed
after `deploy.sh` replaced the canary leaves the previous run's `held`
record. `./go promote` with GO then shifts 100% of traffic to a canary that
was rolled back or never verified.

## Acceptance criteria

Given a record at `held` for version V at 25%
When the Delivery adapter's `status.sh` reports a different canary, or a
weight other than 25
Then `promote` is NO-GO and names the mismatch.

Given a launchpad run that has started deploying
When it is killed before a verdict
Then the record on disk says the release is in progress, not `held`.

## Perimeter

- `core/promote.sh`
- `core/launchpad.sh`
- `Makefile` (`demo-hold`)
- `ci/promote.sh`
