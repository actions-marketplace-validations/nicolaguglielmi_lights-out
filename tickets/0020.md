# 0020 — Rename metrics_text to render_metrics (main-always-green demo, 1 of 3)

- Status: open
- Labels: refactor, app/backend, M30 (main always green demo)
- Found: 2026-11-07, designing `./go demo-mainline` (docs/adr/0056 §7)

## Problem

`meetups/metrics.py`'s `metrics_text()` renders the Prometheus text page,
but its name reads like a getter of some text. `render_metrics` says what
it does.

This ticket is one of the three `./go demo-mainline` runs together with
0021 and 0022. Its perimeter and 0021's are disjoint, and each change
passes its gates alone; together they break main, because 0021 starts
calling the name this ticket removes.

## Acceptance criteria

Given the app
When `GET /metrics` is requested
Then the response is the page `meetups.metrics.render_metrics()` renders,
and `meetups.metrics` has no `metrics_text` any more.

Given that change
When the test suite runs (`./go test`)
Then a test asserts the rendered page carries
`http_server_request_duration_seconds_count`.

## Perimeter

- `app/backend/meetups/metrics.py`
- `app/backend/meetups/app.py`
- `app/backend/tests/test_metrics.py`

## Notes for the implementer

- Low risk: no migration, no auth, no platform path.
- At L0 the change is a hand-written fixture (`fixtures/0020/dev-worker/`,
  `run.json` says `hand_written: true`); the gates, the tower and the
  main check that judge it are real.
