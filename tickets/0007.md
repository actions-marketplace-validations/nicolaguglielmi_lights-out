# 0007 — A PR is judged by its own gates and its own merge policy

- Status: closed (ADR 0033)
- Labels: platform, core/steps/ci.sh, policy/, security, needs an ADR
- Found: 2026-11-02, audit of core/ before the public launch

## Problem

`core/steps/ci.sh` runs `make gates` inside the PR head's worktree. The
Makefile, `gates/*.sh`, `policy/merge.env` and `core/risk.py` that judge the
PR are the PR's own. A PR that edits `policy/merge.env` (for example adds
every level to `RISK_AUTO_MERGE_LEVELS`, or empties `RISK_HIGH_PLATFORM`)
routes itself to auto-merge. `RISK_HIGH_PLATFORM` lists `policy/*`, but the
list that classifies the PR is the PR's own copy of it. The perimeter check
runs in `core/crew.sh` only, not again in the tower's CI step.

## Acceptance criteria

Given a PR that changes `policy/merge.env` so that its own risk would be
auto-merge
When the tower's CI step judges it
Then the gates and the policy come from the base, the PR is classified
high-risk (platform), and it goes to review, not to auto-merge.

Given a PR whose diff leaves the ticket's `## Perimeter`
When the tower's CI step judges it
Then it is NO-GO, whoever produced the diff.

## Perimeter

- `core/steps/ci.sh`
- `Makefile` (`gates`)
- `docs/adr/` (one new ADR)
- `ci/tower.sh`
