## Problem

osv-scanner reports 27 advisory findings in the app's lockfiles, none
introduced by a PR: they were published after the M4 baseline against
packages the app already pinned. Since docs/adr/0032 they no longer
block a PR, but `gates/deps.sh` lists them on every run:

- `app/backend/uv.lock`: `aiohttp 3.14.3` (fixed in 3.14.4), `gitpython
  3.1.62` (fixed in 3.2.1).
- `app/frontend/package-lock.json`: `vite 8.3.0` (fixed in 8.3.3, dev),
  `source-map-js 1.2.1` (fixed in 1.2.2, dev).

The bump is not a one-line change. `app/fixtures/gates/vulnerable-dep/`'s
patch adds its entry next to `"vite": "8.3.0"` in `package-lock.json`, so
bumping vite moves the patch's context and the fixture stops applying.
Other fixtures and the recorded dev-worker runs may carry lockfile
context as well.

## Acceptance criteria

Given the app's lockfiles at HEAD
When `gates/deps.sh` runs on any fixture
Then it reports 0 inherited findings for these four packages.

Given every fixture under `app/fixtures/gates/`
When `./go demo-gates` runs
Then each one applies and gets the verdict its `expect` declares.

Given the recorded runs under `fixtures/`
When `./go demo` runs at L0 on a clean container
Then it prints `GO: demo`.
