**lights-out 0.7.0** turns a client's raw request into tickets, shows each ticket's whole path at a glance, and publishes what the agents did on a real codebase, numbers and stops included.

- **The intake interview** ([ADR 0090](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0090-the-intake-interview.md)). `./go intake` takes a request in free text. The product role asks one question at a time, at most five, then proposes tickets with Given/When/Then criteria, a feature and a priority. The architect proposes each perimeter from the workload's files. A person confirms, edits or drops each ticket, and the spec gate judges the confirmed ones, unchanged. Every ADK role now has a turn cap, `ADK_MAX_TURNS`, and a capped run is in the spend ledger.
- **The flow strip** ([ADR 0085](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0085-the-flow-strip.md)). Every card carries its ticket's path in one row: the request, the perimeter, the eight gates in the order the tower runs them, the PR, the canary, the human gate and 100%. Each node shows its verdict, its time and its reason.
- **The role matrix** ([ADR 0086](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0086-the-role-matrix.md)). The six roles side by side: provider, harness, thinking and tokens against the budget.
- **The burn and the run in flight** ([ADR 0087](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0087-burn-projection-and-the-run-in-flight.md)). `./go spend` and the console show the spend per hour, the day's projection against its limit, and the attempt in flight against `TOKEN_BUDGET`, with a warning from 80%. A run stopped by its budget is in the ledger. Gemini's thinking tokens are priced as output.
- **The provider scoreboard** ([ADR 0088](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0088-the-provider-scoreboard.md)). `./go scoreboard` puts the providers side by side from sourced results. It is seeded with M24's ten tenacity tickets: 10 of 10 merged on Anthropic, 7 of 10 on Gemini with three `TOKEN_BUDGET` stops, and OpenAI not run. It is published on the site, and it is not a benchmark.
- **A read-only console snapshot** ([ADR 0089](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0089-a-read-only-console-snapshot.md)). `./go evidence` freezes the console of a real run into one signed, self-contained page. The site links the latest one.
- **Adapters outside the repository** ([ADR 0058](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0058-outside-adapters-and-other-cis-as-surfaces.md)), admitted by the exact hash of their tree and a conformance GO. GitLab can be the code host (`./go gitlab-conformance`), and Jenkins is a surface that shows the gates' line and never judges (`./go demo-jenkins`).
- **The gates' logic in Python** ([ADR 0084](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.7.0/docs/adr/0084-gates-under-the-shell-boundary.md)). Ten gates moved to `core/gates/`, with unit tests. Shell in `gates/` went from 4,074 to 2,489 lines.
