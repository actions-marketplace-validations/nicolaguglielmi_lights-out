**lights-out 0.6.0** shows why a gate said no, puts the one human decision on the card, and measures what delivery costs and how fast it goes.

- **The why of a NO-GO, one click away** ([ADR 0078](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.6.0/docs/adr/0078-the-why-of-a-no-go-one-click-away.md)). Every rejected attempt carries its gate, its line, its details and, for `sast`, the new findings. A click or Enter on a red gate opens one panel with the attempt, the attempts left and the next move. The details stay withheld until that attempt's `secrets` gate said GO.
- **The human gate on the card** ([ADR 0079](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.6.0/docs/adr/0079-the-human-gate-on-the-card.md)). The held release's card is the one card that drags, onto "Promote to 100%" or "Rollback". The drop opens the same confirmation panel as the launch screen; nothing moves without it, a NO-GO needs a reason, and the record keeps the reason and the brief that was answered.
- **The live canary** ([ADR 0080](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.6.0/docs/adr/0080-the-live-canary.md)). Each stage records when it started, when it was judged and its numbers; the console shows the stage in course and, on a rollback, its reason.
- **An evidence pack per release** ([ADR 0081](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.6.0/docs/adr/0081-the-evidence-pack-per-release.md)). `./go evidence VERSION=<v>` writes one signed file with the release, its stages, the image and SBOM digests, the DAST line, the human answer and the PR's gates, each value with its source and every gap named.
- **The delivery measures** ([ADR 0082](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.6.0/docs/adr/0082-delivery-measures-from-the-logbook.md)). `./go dora` gives DORA's four keys over a window, from the logbook; a measure the record cannot give is printed as not measured, with its reason.
- **Cost and time per ticket, with a budget** ([ADR 0083](https://github.com/nicolaguglielmi/lights-out/blob/v0.6.0/docs/adr/0083-cost-and-time-per-ticket-with-a-budget.md)). `./go spend` prices each run from a dated table in `profiles/prices.json`. `SPEND_BUDGET_TICKET_USD` and `SPEND_BUDGET_DAY_USD` stop the next attempt and send the ticket to needs-human with the budget's line on its card. Off by default.
- **The console's Overview** shows the delivery measures and the spend in the words of `./go dora` and `./go spend`. The logbook is at 1.5.
