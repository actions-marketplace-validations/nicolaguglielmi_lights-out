We run the meetups site for our community. People sign up for a meetup and
then find they can't make it, and there is no way to say so: their seat stays
taken, the meetup looks full when it isn't, and the organiser ends up keeping
a separate list of who is really coming.

We'd like members to be able to give their place back, and the organiser to
see who is actually coming and how many seats are still free. Nobody should be
able to cancel someone else's place. We don't need emails or notifications
for now.
